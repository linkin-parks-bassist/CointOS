# Lead Agent Integration with Other Roles

## Relationship with Worker Role

The Lead agent works closely with Worker agents by:
- Reviewing completed work before final approval
- Providing feedback when work doesn't meet requirements
- Ensuring proper handoff documentation is provided
- Validating that all required steps have been completed

## Relationship with Intake Role

The Lead agent coordinates with Intake agents by:
- Ensuring tasks are properly defined before work begins
- Validating that task requirements are clear and complete
- Confirming that intake decisions are properly documented

## Relationship with Steward Role

The Lead agent works alongside Stewards by:
- Supporting ecosystem maintenance through proper task completion
- Ensuring quality standards are maintained across all tasks
- Following steward principles of documentation and verification

## Relationship with Verifier Role

The Lead agent complements Verifiers by:
- Focusing on process and handoff compliance
- Ensuring documentation quality and completeness
- Supporting verification through proper preparation and organization

## Integration Points

The Lead agent serves as a bridge between:
- Initial task definition and completion
- Worker execution and final approval
- Quality assurance and ecosystem maintenance
- Process compliance and deliverable quality
