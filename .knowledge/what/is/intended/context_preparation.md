Task context should contain the governing brief and small verified facts needed to
begin; durable knowledge remains discoverable through semantic knowledge roots.
Workers should not receive whole historical plans, reports, transcripts, or document
trees. A coordinator may prepare task-specific context, but must distinguish verified
facts from assumptions and avoid duplicating orientation already supplied by the
mandatory knowledge-tree skills. Any future teaching or advisory role contributes
verified reusable knowledge through the same tree rather than creating a parallel
memory system.
