CointOS runs local OpenCode agents through pre-emptible model lanes. cointosd owns scheduling, durable assignments, receipts, validation and publication; Coin supplies Telegram intake/control. Configured native adapters control model execution; Lemonade remains an optional machine-level service. Source and installed runtime are distinct.

Tasks and queues are durable desired work; runs, thoughts, units and caches are execution state. Checked receipts/acceptance settle work. Conversations survive ordinary interruption; disposable cached tokens must match the next prompt. The daemon is the sole ledger writer; damaged authority refuses mutation/adoption.

Managers plan bounded pipelines, workers submit artifacts and integrators review exact candidates. Reviewed hidden tests expose failures to their implementers. The work frontier derives one recovery owner per canonical assignment/version; failed attempts do not create manager-to-Operator chains. Managers atomically hand off runnable children. Recovery replace checks and completes handoff to existing work in one transaction. correct-tests also completes checked recovery with its independent correction request. Accepted test contracts can be independently corrected, preserving prior acceptance and implementation artifacts until the new review passes. Planning cannot publish product changes. Publication preserves editor work while atomically advancing accepted refs.
