1. A manager decomposes the relevant specification into small observable behaviors, retaining later requirements in the product frontier.
2. Skeleton workers establish the interfaces, ownership/error contracts and build seams needed by the subpart.
3. A separate test-mapping manager publishes single-line natural-language scenarios that collectively enforce the scoped specification.
4. An independent map-review manager reads the specification and checks each obligation against the scenario list's concrete conditions and expected observations. Its published assessment identifies the reviewed list and spec revision.
5. On acceptance, the reviewer hands off one scenario to each test author. Each author uses the reviewed line, spec sources and established APIs/fixtures.
6. Independent implementers receive the same scoped behavioral contract and depend on its reviewed executable test packets.
7. Integrators review exact candidates and join accepted pieces through bounded integration work.
