Worker items target about five useful work-model minutes, excluding lane waits and tools. Size a coherent behavior or interface boundary with checkable output, named context and resolved design choices. File/function counts are hints, not reasons to split a cohesive change. Managers expose the broader product shape but elaborate only the next boundary within their pinned allowance.

A child brief states outcome, exclusions, input/output, errors, ownership, affected area, prerequisites and acceptance. Independent test/implementation chains share one behavior contract. Higher integration joins already-tested boundaries. Preserve the unexpanded product frontier in the plan; scope completion requires judgment, not parsed plan prose.

Managers use atomic `handoff` to queue runnable child assignments and complete planning together. Ordinary waits are valid; dead dependencies refuse the whole handoff. Queue records retain proposed_by ownership. Product children continue independently, without polling continuation managers.

Workers independently report contradictory or oversized assignments as blocked with evidence. A single recovery controller verifies the obstruction and uses revision, replacement, reviewed test correction or infrastructure repair to restore progress. It preserves branches and receipts. An unchanged assignment does not gain an automatic chain of manager/Operator allowances.

Config owns generation budgets and role_fresh_retries. Workers, managers, integrators and Operators default to zero fresh refills; interruption recovery remains distinct. Calibration must be judged by useful completed work. The faster MiniCPM trial did not establish reliable manager/reviewer judgment.
