Native adherence to the mapping/review handoffs remains unverified.

It publishes a focused product knowledge answer with the scope and exact spec sources above a list. Each item is one natural-language line describing a concrete setup/action and observable expected result. The collection expresses the full scoped intention: successful behavior, boundaries, failure outcomes, ownership and ordering as applicable. Reference accepted existing coverage by exact test identity and record fixture/interface prerequisites with their owners.
