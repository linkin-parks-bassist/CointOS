Distil `ecosystem/executor.py` by obligation, not by evenly dividing its roughly 2,000 lines. It composes process-gate ownership, physical worker/inference/proxy admission and exact close, durable job persistence/recovery, usage observation, and post-close job outcome policy. A split is useful only when it clarifies a contract and removes the old semantic route; moving functions while retaining executor as an ambient service locator would not help.

The key vertical boundary is physical close versus logical continuation. `close_runner_round` establishes exact process/backend/lease closure or leaves `reconciliation_required`; it does not decide cancellation, budget continuation, preemption, verification, or acceptance. Cancellation must outrank budget and preemption after verified close. `_complete_requested_cancellation` owns the immediate, late-preemption, and runner-exit checks.

`ecosystem/job_outcomes.py` now owns two pure post-close record transformations: `budget_checkpoint_job` returns either a ready retained-session continuation with round usage reset or a terminal no-session checkpoint; `preempted_job` returns a ready record with observed session availability, reason, count, and cleared pending marker. Both take the timestamp as data and leave the input job unchanged. The executor still owns durable cancellation checks, resource-interruption retention, persistence, audit, and physical-close ordering; `_persist_job` reconciles a concurrent resource interruption before publication. Dead-claim cancellation recovery, post-close replay, and notification-intent repair are installed; source and installed executor copies match after a no-restart deployment. Four quick pure tests and seven temporary-root branch tests cover the transition contract and ordering; current standard `tests/` discovery passes 837 tests, and the separate process-integration discovery passes 33. These are not live emergency or sibling-concurrency qualification.

The executor now persists generation-bound round-outcome evidence before close and replays dead-owner post-close records only with matching physical-close evidence. Terminal job state and result-notification recipient intent now share a durable job write, with separate idempotent outbox reconciliation. The remaining outcome branches still need a coherent plain-data contract, and external delivery ambiguity must stay distinct from durable intent. Keep exact job ID, generation, session evidence, cancellation marker, close result and usage; do not spread persistence or audit among outcome functions. Extract process-gate ownership only if all consumers can call a clear kernel-facing interface directly, without wrappers, mirrored state, or callback choreography. Keep KT startup proofs side-effect-free and subsecond; temporary-root integration tests belong in the repository suite.
