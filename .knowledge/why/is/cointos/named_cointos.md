The name evolved through `Cointelprofessional` → `Cointelpro` → `Coin` →
`CoinToss`, whose sound suggested CentOS and therefore `CointOS`. The operating
system claim is intentional: agents are persistent logical processes, GPU time is
scheduled execution, memory and disk provide residency tiers, and tools and
messaging are I/O at the agent layer.

`CointOS` names the whole environment. `Cointelprofessional` names the permanent
Telegram control-plane identity; its shortened forms do not denote separate agents.
