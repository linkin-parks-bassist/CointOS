They do not. Role prompts must not impose arbitrary minute, attempt or fallback-marker limits: elapsed time and attempt count do not prove completion. An agent works until its step is done or a concrete blocker requires a resumable handoff. Scope, cancellation, resource priority, acceptance conditions and concrete blockers remain valid boundaries. An explicit limit supplied by the task (for example a scan limit) is preserved.
