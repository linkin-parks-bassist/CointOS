**Publication and recovery.** CointOS captures, prepares and publishes exact artifacts through checked tools. It preserves editor drafts and other assignments' work. A busy publication refuses for retry. Verified published intents settle after restart without another model review; holds and changed assignments revoke stale validation results. Transport success alone never accepts product work.

Managers and tree maintenance publish scoped Markdown knowledge. Project Operators may also publish configured non-code maintenance; product code and protected tests/harnesses require worker submission and independent review. check_work reports forbidden changes before publication. Keep original files when preparation refuses.

Pending canonical knowledge edits, deletions and new answers can block overlapping publication. Body, expiry and proof-policy changes are protected; routine lifecycle timestamps alone are not. Preserve the owning answer rather than overwriting it. Gardening defers unavailable answers; they remain visible in health evidence. Completion rechecks selected answers even when no edits were needed: unavailable owners require blocked completion rather than a verified-current claim.

Tests must build and execute in fresh invocation-owned directories and clean up their own outputs. Shared hardcoded executables can produce false passes under interference. Protected harness repairs require normal independent test-contract review.
