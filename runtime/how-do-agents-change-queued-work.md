**Changing queued work.** A project's running manager, control-enabled Operator (or the user) can hold an item with `cointos hold PROJECT:ITEM`: it will not start, and a run of it now stops without losing its branch or attempts. `cointos revise PROJECT:ITEM "COMPLETE BRIEF" --reason "WHY"` (optionally `--stage`, `--reasoning-effort`, `--generation-seconds/--generation-tokens`) replaces what the item asks for, including its `Depends on:` line; omitted stage and reasoning retain their previous values, and supplied budget fields merge with existing limits; its task restarts fresh on the new brief, keeping its branch, and a reviewed item goes back to its worker. Existing waiting integrators wait until their worker is back in review and unheld; a changed brief refreshes their assignment and conversation while preserving their branch/files and explicit admission holds. `cointos hold PROJECT:ITEM --release` lifts a hold; a task's own hold also ends on its changed revision or settlement. Revisions preserve other owners' holds; user holds require explicit user release. Accepted work cannot be revised. Use cointos_correct_tests for an invalid accepted test contract; its original acceptance survives until independent correction review. `cointos cancel PROJECT:ITEM... --reason "WHY"` (the owning manager, control-enabled Operator or the user) withdraws unaccepted items for good: it stops their runs, integrations and recovery controllers, drops their records and retires their branches; dependents must be cancelled in the same command or revised first. A fresh run of an assignment is told which items earlier runs of it already queued. `cointos move PROJECT:ITEM top|up|down|bottom` reorders an item within its project's queue; the spawner admits waiting work in that order. Ordinary roles report missing operations; assigned repair Operators may investigate explicitly named CointOS paths.
