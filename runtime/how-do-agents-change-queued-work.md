**Changing queued work.** Use declared queue controls within your assigned scope. The user or a running agent with current control authority can hold an item with `cointos hold PROJECT:ITEM`: it will not start, and a run of it now stops without losing its files or attempts. `cointos revise PROJECT:ITEM "COMPLETE BRIEF" --reason "WHY"` (optionally `--stage`, `--reasoning-effort`, `--generation-tokens`) replaces what the item asks for, including its `Depends on:` line; a prior hold is not required; omitted stage and reasoning retain their previous values, and supplied budget fields merge with existing limits; a changed assignment restarts its task fresh while preserving its files, and a reviewed item goes back to its worker. The response reports whether the assignment changed. An unchanged revision restarts nothing and completes no recovery handoff. Existing waiting integrators wait until their worker is back in review and unheld; a changed brief refreshes their assignment and conversation while preserving their files and explicit admission holds. `cointos hold PROJECT:ITEM --release` lifts a hold; a task's own hold also ends on its changed revision or settlement. Revisions preserve other owners' holds; user holds require explicit user release. Accepted work cannot be revised. Use cointos_correct_tests for an invalid accepted test contract; its original acceptance survives until independent correction review. `cointos cancel PROJECT:ITEM... --reason "WHY"` (subject to current control authority) withdraws unaccepted items for good: it stops their runs, integrations and First Responders, withdraws their queue records and preserves recoverable work; dependents must be cancelled in the same command or revised first. Fresh launches point to current task inspection rather than copying prior queue history. `cointos move PROJECT:ITEM top|up|down|bottom` reorders an item within its project's queue; the spawner admits waiting work in that order. Ordinary roles report missing operations; assigned First Responders may investigate explicitly named CointOS paths.
